CGST + SGST

How GST is split between Centre and State

For intrastate transactions (buyer and seller in the same state), GST is split equally between Central GST (CGST) and State GST (SGST).

18% GST on ₹1,000 base price:
CGST (9%) = ₹90
SGST (9%) = ₹90
Total GST = ₹180

For interstate transactions, IGST (full 18%) applies instead of CGST+SGST.

FAQ

Common questions

What are the GST slabs in India?
India has four main GST slabs: 5% for essential goods, 12% for processed goods, 18% for most goods and services (the most common), and 28% for luxury and demerit goods. There is also a 0% rate for essential items like unprocessed agricultural produce and healthcare services.
What is the difference between CGST and SGST?
In intrastate (within the same state) transactions, GST is split equally between Central GST (CGST, collected by the central government) and State GST (SGST, collected by the state government). On 18% GST: 9% is CGST and 9% is SGST. For interstate transactions, IGST (the full rate) applies and is later divided between states.
How do I remove GST from a GST-inclusive price?
Base price = Inclusive price ÷ (1 + GST rate/100). For example, ₹1,180 inclusive of 18% GST: Base = 1180 ÷ 1.18 = ₹1,000. GST = ₹180. Our calculator handles this automatically — just select "Remove GST" mode.
Is GST applicable on all goods and services in India?
Most goods and services are covered under GST. Exemptions include fresh vegetables, milk, eggs, unbranded food grain, healthcare services, education, and a few others. Petroleum products, alcohol for human consumption, and electricity are not under GST and are taxed by states separately.
