EMI formula

How EMI is calculated

EMI = P × r × (1+r)ⁿ / ((1+r)ⁿ - 1)
P = Principal loan amount (e.g. ₹50,00,000)
r = Monthly interest rate = Annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100 (e.g. 8.5% ÷ 12 ÷ 100 = 0.00708)
n = Total number of monthly installments (e.g. 20 years × 12 = 240)

FAQ

Common questions

What is EMI?
EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) is the fixed amount you pay every month to repay a loan. Each payment covers both the interest on the outstanding principal and a portion of the principal itself. Early in the loan, most of each EMI goes toward interest; later, more goes toward the principal.
How can I reduce my EMI?
Three ways: (1) Increase tenure — longer tenure means lower monthly EMI but higher total interest paid. (2) Make a larger down payment — reduces the principal, which directly reduces EMI. (3) Negotiate a lower interest rate — even 0.5% reduction on a large home loan saves a significant amount in total interest.
Is this EMI calculator accurate for Indian banks?
Yes. The calculator uses the standard EMI formula used by all Indian banks and financial institutions. The result will match your bank's calculation. Note that your actual EMI statement may include processing fees, GST on interest, or insurance premiums not included in this basic calculation.
Is my financial data stored?
No. All calculations happen locally in your browser. Your loan amount, interest rate, and tenure are never sent to any server.
